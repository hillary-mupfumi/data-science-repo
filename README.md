## Projects

1. [Telecom Customer Churn Prediction](https://github.com/hillary-mupfumi/data-science-repo/tree/main/Customer%20Churn%20Prediction-%20Telecoms)
    - **Description:** The aim of this project is to analyze customer demographics, services, tenure and other variables to predict whether a particular customer will churn or not.
    - **Technologies Used:** The notebooks use Decision Tree Classifier, Random Forest Classifier and K Nearest Neighbor Classifier.
    - **Results:** Recall was used since the class was imbalanced. The decision was the best model, achieving a recall score of 64%.

2. [Inventory Demand Forecasting](https://github.com/hillary-mupfumi/data-science-repo/tree/main/Inventory%20Demand%20Forecasting)
    - **Description:** Daily unit demand for a beverage distributor's highest-volume SKU is forecast and translated into an operational reorder point and stockout risk signal.
    - **Technologies Used:** XGBoost regression with calendar and lag features, benchmarked against a seasonal-naive baseline.
    - **Results:** Mean absolute error was reduced by 20.15% relative to the baseline, with a mean absolute percentage error of 11.95% on a 90-day holdout period.

3. [RFM Customer Segmentation](https://github.com/hillary-mupfumi/data-science-repo/tree/main/RFM%20Customer%20Segmentation)
    - **Description:** Customers are segmented using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary behaviour derived from two years of transaction history, to prioritize retention effort.
    - **Technologies Used:** Rule-based RFM quartile scoring, validated against k-means clustering on standardized RFM values.
    - **Results:** Two segments representing 48.08% of customers were found to account for 86.18% of total revenue.

4. [A/B Testing / Experimentation Analysis](https://github.com/hillary-mupfumi/data-science-repo/tree/main/AB%20Testing%20Experimentation%20Analysis)
    - **Description:** A checkout page redesign is evaluated for its effect on conversion rate and average order value using a randomized experiment.
    - **Technologies Used:** Two-proportion z-test, two-sample t-test, and post-hoc power analysis.
    - **Results:** The redesign produced a statistically significant 10.37% relative lift in conversion rate (p = 0.0016), consistent across all device segments tested.
